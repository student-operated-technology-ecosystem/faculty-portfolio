10 · ANTICIPATED PROFESSIONAL GROWTH

What's Next

My next stage of professional growth centers on expanding experiential technical education, strengthening infrastructure-based learning, and continuing to connect curriculum with the tools and practices students will encounter in professional IT environments.

Expand the Student-Operated Technology Ecosystem

A primary goal is to continue developing SOTE from a framework into a durable student-operated technical environment. That includes deeper integration of networking, server and virtualization infrastructure, cybersecurity, help-desk operations, documentation, knowledge management, and research-and-development work.

The long-term objective is an ecosystem in which courses can use shared professional infrastructure while students progressively assume meaningful operational responsibilities.

Develop the Infrastructure Layer

Continued work with virtualization, servers, networking, and related infrastructure will support both curriculum development and SOTE. Building and documenting these environments provides a technical foundation for more advanced applied learning while also requiring continued professional development in the technologies being taught.

Next-stage development
Build the platform → develop the curriculum → put students into authentic roles → evaluate what the environment makes possible.

Advance Networking & Server Curriculum

Future curriculum development will continue in networking and server administration, with an emphasis on translating infrastructure concepts into applied, observable work. The goal is to extend the same philosophy now visible in CIT-205: students should encounter technical concepts through environments and workflows that resemble professional practice.

Continue Research-Informed Course Design

My collaboration with Carnegie Mellon University and CCAC through the NSF Future of Work project has connected curriculum mapping, learning science, and AI-supported educational technologies. I intend to continue applying those ideas to technical curriculum development and to evaluate where emerging tools can improve learning without replacing the authentic problem solving students need to develop.

Develop Students as Contributors

Continued growth of SOTE will create additional opportunities for students to move beyond completing assignments and begin contributing to documentation, infrastructure, validation, support, knowledge, and improvement work. Developing those pathways is both a curriculum goal and a professional-growth goal because it requires new approaches to supervision, assessment, workflow design, and student responsibility.

Near-Term Priorities

  • Continue development of SOTE infrastructure and operational workflows
  • Expand virtualization and server environments for applied instruction
  • Develop and refine advanced networking and server curriculum
  • Extend the CIT-205 experiential model through authentic service-desk and knowledge workflows
  • Continue research-informed experimentation with AI-supported learning
  • Create additional pathways for students to participate in meaningful technical operations
  • Document and evaluate the resulting work for continued curriculum improvement
