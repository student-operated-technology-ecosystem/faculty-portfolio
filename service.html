09 · SERVICE

Service to the College & Community

My service work connects curriculum, institutional capacity, workforce development, student opportunity, and external partnerships. Much of that work centers on building environments in which education and real technical practice reinforce one another.

Center for Education, Innovation & Training

I played a central role in the development and launch of CCAC's Center for Education, Innovation & Training (CEIT), contributing to stakeholder engagement, industry partnerships, fundraising and grant development, program planning, equipment and laboratory strategy, and government and community outreach surrounding the facility.

My continuing work in CEIT supports technology curriculum, industry partnerships, laboratories, certification programming, and advanced technical learning. This service extends beyond an individual course by helping build the institutional environment in which technical programs and students operate.

Student-Operated Technology Ecosystem

I developed the Student-Operated Technology Ecosystem (SOTE) as a framework for transforming production-style technical environments into structured learning and service opportunities. The CCAC implementation includes networking, server and virtualization environments, cybersecurity infrastructure, help-desk/support operations, wiring and validation spaces, documentation systems, and research-and-development work.

SOTE also creates opportunities for students to contribute to systems larger than a single class assignment. Students can participate in validation, documentation, infrastructure, support, knowledge management, and operational improvement while developing professional habits and leaving useful work behind for the institution and future students.

Service through infrastructure
Build institutional capacity → create student opportunity → document the work → leave the system stronger.

Industry Certification & Faculty Development

I helped establish CEIT as a platform for NC3 industry certification and instructor professional development and was heavily involved in planning the center's first NC3 instructor professional-development / Train-the-Trainer event, held November 13–15, 2023. The three-day program brought more than 34 participants from 21 organizations nationwide.

This work positioned CCAC not only as a consumer of professional development, but as a host and convening site for educators and industry-aligned technical training.

Workforce & Industry Partnerships

My CCAC service has included work supporting partnerships that connect technical education with workforce pathways. This includes involvement with Google TechWise, Johnson Controls-related mechatronics and building-technology development, industry certification programming, and collaborative projects with Carnegie Mellon University.

These partnerships bring external expertise, resources, mentoring, research, and career context into the college while also creating opportunities for CCAC to contribute to regional workforce and educational initiatives.

Research & Cross-Institutional Collaboration

As a Co-Principal Investigator on the NSF Future of Work project A New Bridge to the Digital Economy: Integrated AI-Augmented Learning and Collaboration, I worked within a Carnegie Mellon University and CCAC partnership focused on community-college IT curriculum, learning science, AI-supported learning, and access to digital-economy careers.

I also served as Co-Principal Investigator on the Richard King Mellon Foundation project Bridges to Digital Economy Jobs, another Carnegie Mellon University and CCAC collaboration supporting science and technology talent development.

Public & Community Engagement

Service has also included representing CCAC in public and stakeholder-facing settings. Documented examples include participation in CEIT opening programming, a 2022 Our Region's Business appearance discussing the Johnson Controls partnership and technical career pathways, and support for advanced-technology demonstrations during Governor Josh Shapiro's 2024 visit to CEIT.
